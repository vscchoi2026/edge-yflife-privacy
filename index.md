# Edge-YFLife Privacy Policy

**App Version:** 1.2.0 (build 3)  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026

---

## Overview

Edge-YFLife is a financial planning application designed for insurance agents. The app operates **entirely offline** on your iPad, with no internet connectivity required for core functionality.

---

## Data Collection & Storage

### What We Collect
- **None.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

### What We Store Locally
- **License Code:** Your activation code is stored locally on your device.
- **App State:** Your current planning session state (if applicable).
- **Device Information:** Only what's necessary for the app to function (device ID, OS version).

### Data Sharing
- **No third-party sharing.** The app does not transmit data to any external servers.

---

## License Code

Your license code is:
- Generated locally when you install the app.
- Stored locally on your device.
- Valid for **2 years** from the date of issuance (expires November 2, 2028).
- Can be re-entered if you reinstall the app (contact support for reissuance).
